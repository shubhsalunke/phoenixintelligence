DATA GOVERNANCE & COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Compliant with Global Cyber Defense & LEA Data Standards

1. Overview & Commitment

Phoenix Cyber Intelligence Platform ("Phoenix", "we", "our") is dedicated to upholding the strictest standards of data protection, confidential intelligence handling, and ethical cybersecurity protocols. This Privacy Policy details the types of information processed through our cyber threat intelligence interfaces and portal.

2. Information We Collect

We process information strictly in the context of authorized enterprise cyber defense operations and inquiries:

  • Access Inquiries & Officer Verification: Name, official agency email, department/unit identification, and jurisdiction details provided when requesting platform clearances.
  • Audit & Telemetry Logs: Timestamped IP access logs, cryptographic session tokens, and query hash signatures maintained solely for tamper-evident LEA operational audit trails.
  • Threat Reconnaissance Metadata: OSINT artifacts, network indicators of compromise (IoCs), and dark web vulnerability indices aggregated during analytical scans.

3. Data Protection & Security Standards

All data processed within Phoenix is secured using enterprise-grade defense mechanisms:

  • Zero-Knowledge Encryption: AES-256 GCM encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.
  • Air-Gapped Compliance: Support for sovereign on-premise deployments with zero telemetry egress.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Multi-factor authenticated clearance enforcement for all analysts.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure Policy

We do not sell, rent, or commercialize any intelligence data. Information is disclosed strictly under authorized judicial warrants, statutory law enforcement mandates, or explicit directives by the authorized subscribing agency.
